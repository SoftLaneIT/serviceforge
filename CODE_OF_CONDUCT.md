# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus on what is best for the community and platform.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, and other interactions that violate this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to: security@softlaneit.com

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible.
